The [2026 brief](https://community.element14.com/challenges-projects/halloween/b/news/posts/join-the-element14-community-2026-halloween-hackathon-create-compete-for-prizes) requests a single write-up explaining the build and Halloween connection, with image/video demonstrations; it names originality, innovation and technical merit. The page lists a closing time of 23:59 UK on 15 November 2026. Check the current page and complete terms before submitting; this package does not establish eligibility.

## Our quality bar

A concise narrative, actual photographs, one coherent physical game, six readable diagrams, a machine-readable netlist, source attachments, and an honest fault/validation record. Avoid fabricated measurements, simulated images presented as photographs, unsupported complete-win claims and missing media presented as a working link. The unreviewed gameplay recording is the remaining publication dependency.
