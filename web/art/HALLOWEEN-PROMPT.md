## Final prompt

Use case: stylized-concept. Asset type: original Halloween illustration for a vintage haunted telephone exchange game. Wide horizontal 3:2 composition, beautifully drawn 1906 Halloween postcard / antique chromolithograph with woodcut linework. A black antique telephone in an abandoned municipal exchange surrounded by three intricately carved glowing jack-o-lanterns, wax candles, curling telephone cords turning into little sheet ghosts, tiny bats against an enormous pale full moon seen through a gothic window. Rich burnt pumpkin orange, bone ivory, midnight plum, soot black. Distressed printed paper, hand-inked hatching, imperfect offset registration, beautifully intentional detailed illustration. Eerie and playful but sophisticated, not photorealistic, not 3D, no gradients in the graphic shapes, no modern glossy UI. Central telephone silhouette clear; darkest shadows around outer edges for easy placement in a dark website. No words, no lettering, no border, no watermark. This is atmospheric decorative art for Halloween, not a UI mockup.

## In-game decorations

web/halloween.js draws pumpkins, candles, cobwebs and bats directly in Canvas. Each completed caller lights the carved pumpkin on its destination. The green/yellow/red operator warnings retain their original meanings. Candle movement becomes static when reduced motion is enabled.
